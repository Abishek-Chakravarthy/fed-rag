## Administrative Notes:

1. **Group project <MAXIMUM 3 MEMBERS IN A GROUP>** students can present at the same time. Please let me know by email (by 16/01/2026 - 5 PM) if you are doing a group project. Also, mention the group member’s name, roll number and your supervisor’s name. Email from only one member of the group is fine.

2. Those who are doing the final project under external guidance (Not the One allocated by the Department), you have to take approval from your internal guide. You must inform your Internal Guide what project you are going to do and its scope. It’s totally your Internal Guide’s choice to allow you to do the project under external guidance or not. If allowed by your Internal Guide, please let me know by email (by 16/01/2026 - 5 PM). In the email you must attach the approval copy obtained from your Internal Guide. If you are signing an NDA with your external Guide/Company then that work can’t be considered as final project. You are supposed to disclose everything during your presentation that you did with an external guide (If permitted).

3. You are required to meet with your Guide Each Week (At least Once) and Get his/her signature in the attached weekly review report, without which you will not be allowed to present your work for Mid/End review.

## Evaluation/Grading Notes:

### 1. Components of evaluation:

a. **Internal Guide/supervisor** (30 marks)

b. **Mid Sem Panel Evaluation** (30 marks)

- i. Problem Definition / Literature Survey / Field study / Data gathering / Concept exploration (5 marks)  
- ii. Contribution / Progress made / Work done / Analysis / Prototype / Products developed (10 marks)  
- iii. Presentation (5 marks)  
- iv. Project Report/ Plagiarism Report/ Weekly Review Report (5 marks)  
- v. Viva (5 marks)

c. **End Sem Panel Evaluation** (40 marks)

- i. Problem Definition / Literature Survey / Field study / Data gathering / Concept exploration (5 marks)  
- ii. Contribution / Progress made / Work done / Analysis / Prototype / Products developed (10 marks)  
- iii. Presentation (5 marks)  
- iv. Project Report/ Plagiarism Report/ Weekly Review Report (10 marks)  
- v. Viva (10 marks)

---

2. Absolute grading would be followed. (S:85, A:75, B:65, C:55, D:45, E:35)

3. 10 mins will be allotted to each student (7 mins for presentation with demo + 3 mins for question answering). Please be ready with your code in your laptop, Panel may ask you to show the implementation code.

4. No. of slides should not be more than 15. PPT has to be in the template which will be shared before the Mid Sem/End Sem Evaluation. In the case of Group Project, the no. of slides can go up to 20-25 (for 2 Members) / 30-40 (for 3 Members).

5. Final project report + Presentation + Plagiarism report of the project report (<=10 %) should be uploaded before the Mid Sem and End Sem Evaluation. For obtaining the Plagiarism report, contact your Internal Guide. Separate assignments will be created in the google classroom for all these components in due course of time. Also, templates will be shared for each one in due course of time. Late submission of any of these components may lead to a penalty in grade. The naming convention for the presentation should be strictly followed as `<your_roll_number>.pptx` / `<your_roll_number>.pdf`.

6. For checking the plagiarism, check from the Abstract page onwards. Accordingly, you can crop your original report before checking the plagiarism. Do not include the title page, acknowledgement page and certificate page while checking plagiarism.

7. Hard copy of the report with your guide's (Internal / external (if applicable)) signature needs to be submitted to your Course Coordinator before the presentation (Exact Date will be notified Later). In case of group projects, do not forget to add your individual contribution in your respective report. As this is the Final Report, we are not mentioning any page limit. You can discuss with your guide(s) what and how much contents need to be kept in the report. Reports need to be printed on both sides.

8. Report must contain the 1st page of the plagiarism report (signed by you and your guide) and the image of the weekly review report (format present below) at the end.

9. Signatures of external guide (If applicable) and internal guide are not needed in the Soft Copies (i.e Project Report/ Plagiarism report of the project report) that you will submit using google classroom.

10. In the Hardcopy of the report, ensure the following things:

   a. You have signed in the declaration of originality page.  
   b. Your external guide has signed in the certificate page. (If applicable)  
   c. Your internal guide has signed in the certificate page.  
   d. Image of the weekly review report attached after the reference page.  
   e. You and your internal guide has signed on the 1st page of the plagiarism report (which has to be attached at the end of the report, after the weekly review report page)

11. Guide's signature (internal and external if applicable) on the hard copy of the report is mandatory. Without that you won't be allowed to present your work before the panel.

12. Evaluation will be done from 9 AM to 5 PM on the date as mentioned by the Course Coordinator. Actual timing will be intimated one day before the presentation.

---

## Few Guidelines for preparation of report/presentation

1. Please include citations of different sources properly in both report and presentation.
2. Do not write long sentences on the slide (bullet points would suffice)
3. You can contact your guide on how to organize the report/ presentation.
4. Please show your report and presentation slides to your internal and external supervisor (if applicable), well before the submission deadline.
